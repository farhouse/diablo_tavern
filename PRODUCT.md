# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo players testing a browser-based ARPG management loop in short sessions. They read visitor needs, compare persisted prices, manage a constrained stash, and decide whether to trade or commission a traveler.

## Product Purpose

Diablo Tavern turns loot and character fantasy into a merchant-management game. Success means a player can complete the full visitor loop—arrival, trade, commission, return, claim, departure—without controlling or purchasing a permanent hero roster.

## Brand Personality

Grim, legible, deliberate. The voice is plain and specific, with enough tavern flavor to support the fiction without obscuring consequences.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, casino-like reward noise, ornamental fantasy UI that hides state, and any interface that suggests heroes are units the player hires or directly controls.

## Design Principles

- Make the next valid decision and its consequence visible.
- Treat server state as the only authority for prices, outcomes, and rewards.
- Keep trade information dense but calm enough to compare at a glance.
- Use the tavern fiction to clarify ownership, travel, and risk.
- Preserve recovery paths for full stashes, insufficient gold, and uncertain network responses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, complete keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic status/error announcements, reduced-motion support, and state cues that never depend on color alone.
